# About us

WGD Society is the Warwick campus home of game design, run by students, for students. We support all levels of skill and ambition, from first-timers looking for guidance, to full-fledged devs looking for a community or a team.

#### **What we do**

We run weekly workshop sessions where our executive team is always on hand to help, or to be stunned (as we so often are) by the games or contributions developed by our talented membership. Our design lecture series also runs weekly, where the speaker (from the exec team or membership) gives an invariably fascinating talk about anything which piques their interest.

#### **Our commitments**

- To *you*: we will always encourage expression of yourself, your ideas and your thoughts.
- To *inclusivity*: our events are for anyone; we have zero tolerance for any kind of harrassment, discriminatory language or action against WGD members.
- To *privacy*: we will never place photos or identifying information online without your consent.

See more detail in our [safe spaces policy](/docs/safe-spaces-policy) and our [constitution](https://github.com/wgdsoc/constitution/blob/master/constitution.md).
